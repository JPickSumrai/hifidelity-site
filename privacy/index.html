Privacy Policy — Hi_Fidelity

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. What Hi_Fidelity is

Hi_Fidelity is a browser extension that captures conversations from AI platforms (Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini) and helps you organize, search, and reuse that context. It is built and operated by Pickering Design Group (PDG).

2. What data we collect

Hi_Fidelity captures the text of an AI conversation only when you click the capture button in the extension. It does not capture in the background, and it does not monitor or record pages on its own. A captured conversation includes the messages you sent and the responses you received in that thread.

If you purchase a Hi_Fidelity Pro license, we collect your email address and generate a license key for your account. We also record a monthly count of tokens used, in order to enforce plan limits.

To enforce the per-license device limit (a maximum of 2 devices per license), the extension generates a random identifier for each device you activate and stores it with your license. This identifier is randomly generated by the extension — it is not derived from your hardware, your browser fingerprint, or any personal data — and is used solely to count how many devices a license is active on.

We do not collect your name, browsing history, cookies, analytics, or any personal information beyond what is described above.

3. Where your data is stored

Captured conversations are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage. By default your data stays on your device.

If you purchase a Pro license, your email address, license key, subscription status, monthly usage count, and the identifiers of the devices you have activated (up to 2) are stored in our database, hosted on Supabase, for license management and device-limit enforcement.

4. When data leaves your device

Your conversation text leaves your device only in the following optional cases:

  • AI summarization (Pro). If you activate Hi_Fidelity Pro with a license key, conversation text is sent to our proxy server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate a summary. The text is not retained on our servers after processing. Free accounts do not use this path — they receive locally generated, non-AI summaries.
  • Bring Your Own Key. If you enter your own Anthropic (Claude) API key or your own Google Gemini API key, conversation text is sent directly from your browser to that provider. It never passes through our servers, and your key is stored only on your device.
  • License verification. If a Pro license key is saved in settings, the extension periodically sends the license key and your device identifier to our server to confirm subscription status. No conversation data is included in that request.

You can use Hi_Fidelity without any of the above. Capture and local storage work entirely offline, with no data transmission.

5. Third-party services

  • Google (Gemini API) — the default engine for AI summarization for Pro users via our proxy, and also used directly if you supply your own Gemini key. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — used only if you supply your own Anthropic API key. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing for Pro subscriptions. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Gumroad — alternative payment option for Pro licenses. See Gumroad's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — stores Pro account records (email, license key, usage count, device identifiers). See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Gmail (Google) — used to email your license key to the address you provided at purchase.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. Conversation text is sent only to Google's Gemini API, or to a provider whose key you supplied, and only for the purpose of generating a summary you asked for. Your license key and device identifiers are used only for subscription and device-limit enforcement — never for advertising, profiling, or resale.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your conversations are stored locally in your browser. You can delete any individual session from within the extension at any time. Uninstalling the extension permanently removes all locally stored data.

To keep storage bounded, the extension automatically retains the 100 most recent sessions and removes older ones.

If you cancel a Pro subscription, your account record remains in our database for license management. To request full deletion of your account data, contact us at the address below.

8. Permissions used

  • Storage — to save captured sessions locally in your browser.
  • Unlimited storage — to accommodate users whose saved sessions exceed the default storage limit.
  • Host access to claude.ai, chatgpt.com, and gemini.google.com — to read conversation content on those platforms when you click capture, and to insert saved context into the chat input when you click inject.

9. Security

If you use Bring Your Own Key, your API key is stored locally in your browser and sent directly to that provider — it never passes through our servers. Your Pro license key is stored locally and transmitted to our server over HTTPS only for subscription verification. All communication with external services uses HTTPS.

10. Children's privacy

Hi_Fidelity is not intended for use by children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy, or to request deletion of your account data, contact us at pdg.sumrai@gmail.com.
