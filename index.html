Your AI context is an asset you own.

Capture it on any platform. Carry it to any other.

You build real thinking inside an AI conversation — the constraints, the decisions, the dead ends already ruled out. Then you open a different assistant and start from nothing.

Hi_Fidelity is a browser extension that makes that context portable. Capture a thread from Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini with one click, then inject it into a conversation anywhere else. The continuity is yours, not the platform's.

How it works

  1. CaptureClick capture on a conversation worth keeping. Nothing is recorded in the background — only what you choose, when you choose it.
  2. KeepSessions are stored in your browser, color-coded by source, searchable by keyword or platform. The most recent 100 are retained.
  3. CarryOne click inserts a saved session's context into your current chat, on any supported platform.

What leaves your device

By default, nothing. Capture and storage run entirely in your browser. Conversation text is sent out only if you turn on AI summarization — to our proxy and Google's Gemini API, or directly to a provider whose key you supplied. Nothing is retained on our servers after a summary is generated.
